Location & map information

Location is off. You can plan a route without GPS.

Location alerts work while this page is open. Rule-based analysis uses eligible road or vehicular incidents within 36 months and a 300 m area. One incident is caution; two are moderate. Five or more, or three with enough severity burden, are high risk. Five or more with very high severity burden are critical. High and critical areas receive accident-prone tags. Caution levels consider how often crashes happened, their severity, and how recently they occurred. Routes compare both tagged areas and individual crash history, not live traffic. Fewer recorded events does not guarantee safety.

Requested route endpoints are sent to OSRM. Place searches and pinned-location name lookups use OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Recent-place suggestions stay in this tab.
